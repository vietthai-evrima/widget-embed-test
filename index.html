Questions

What to expect.

Can't find your answer? Our study coordinators are happy to talk it through.

Is taking part safe?

Every study is reviewed and approved by an independent ethics committee before it starts. The study team monitors your health closely and will explain any known risks before you agree to take part.

Does it cost anything?

No. Study visits, tests and the study treatment are free. Many studies also reimburse travel and parking.

Can I change my mind?

Yes. You can leave a study at any time, for any reason, and it will not affect your usual care.

What happens after I apply?

A coordinator contacts you within a few working days to talk about the study and answer your questions. Applying does not commit you to anything.
